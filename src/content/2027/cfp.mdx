The Global Youth AI & STEM Foundation (GYST-AI) is excited to announce 
the inaugural International NextGen Conference on AI in Biomedical 
Sciences (inCABS 2027)! 

inCABS is an international conference created by and for high school 
students interested in AI, medicine, biology, and the future of science. 
It’s a chance for students from around the world to share their research, 
hear new ideas, and connect with others who are just as curious about these 
fields. What makes inCABS different is that you’re not just learning from 
professors or professionals — you’re learning from other high school students
your own age. You’ll get to see what students around the world are researching,
hear different perspectives, and discover the questions and ideas that other
young researchers are exploring.

Whether you’ve been doing research for years or are just getting started, 
inCABS is a space to learn from one another and explore how students your age 
are thinking about the future of AI and biomedical science. We welcome original
research papers exploring the applications, development, and impact of AI in
the biomedical sciences. Whether you’re investigating a new idea, tackling
a real-world problem, or exploring a question you’ve always been curious 
about, inCABS is a place to share your work and learn from other young 
researchers.

## Conference theme

**Artificial Intelligence in Biomedical Sciences**

Artificial intelligence has emerged as one of the most influential technologies
in modern biomedical sciences. From accelerating genomic research and drug 
discovery to improving clinical diagnosis and healthcare delivery, AI is 
redefining how scientific discoveries are made and translated into real-world 
applications. 

The conference welcomes research spanning computational, experimental, ethical, 
engineering, and interdisciplinary perspectives related to AI in biomedical
sciences.
