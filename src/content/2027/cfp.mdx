The Global Youth AI & STEM Foundation (GYST-AI) is pleased to announce the
inaugural International NextGen Conference on AI in Biomedical Sciences
(inCABS 2027).

inCABS is an annual international conference dedicated to showcasing
outstanding research conducted by high school students from around the
world. The conference provides a rigorous academic forum where young
researchers can present original work, exchange ideas, and engage with
peers who share a passion for scientific discovery and technological
innovation.

Artificial intelligence is transforming biomedical sciences by enabling
breakthroughs in healthcare, biological research, drug development, and
medical technologies. As AI continues to reshape these fields, the next
generation of scientists and innovators will play a critical role in
advancing responsible, interdisciplinary research.

The inaugural inCABS welcomes original research papers that explore the
application, development, and implications of artificial intelligence in
biomedical sciences.

## Conference theme

**Artificial Intelligence in Biomedical Sciences**

Artificial intelligence has emerged as one of the most influential
technologies in modern biomedical sciences. From accelerating genomic
research and drug discovery to improving clinical diagnosis and healthcare
delivery, AI is redefining how scientific discoveries are made and
translated into real-world applications.

The conference welcomes research spanning computational, experimental,
ethical, engineering, and interdisciplinary perspectives related to AI in
biomedical sciences.
