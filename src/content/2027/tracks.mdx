AI is transforming biomedical science across an enormous range of fields —
from analyzing medical images and discovering new treatments to exploring 
how AI should be developed and used responsibly in healthcare. Our tracks 
are here to help organize submissions for review, not to limit what you can
research. If your project fits more than one track, that’s completely fine;
there’s no “wrong” choice. Describe your project below, and we’ll help you 
find the best fit — or select a track directly if you already know where your 
research belongs!
