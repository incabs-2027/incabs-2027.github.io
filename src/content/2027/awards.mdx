Every accepted paper represents real research and meaningful work, from 
the questions you chose to investigate, to the methods you developed or 
applied, to the conclusions you reached. The awards below are designed to
recognize the many different ways that research can make an impact: some 
papers introduce innovative ideas, some are communicated exceptionally
well through an oral presentation, some make complex research accessible
through a strong poster, and others bring together fields or perspectives
that are rarely explored side by side.

You do not need to be aiming for an award to submit. Most accepted papers 
will not receive one, and an award is not what determines the value of your
research. Every accepted paper represents a student who took an idea, 
explored it seriously, and shared what they found.
