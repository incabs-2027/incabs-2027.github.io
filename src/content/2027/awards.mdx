Every accepted paper represents real work. That means original questions
asked, methods carried out, and conclusions drawn. The awards below recognize the
range of what that work can look like. Some papers break new ground, some
are presented brilliantly out loud, some communicate complex ideas clearly
on a poster, and some connect fields that do not usually talk to each
other.

You do not need to be chasing an award to submit. Most accepted authors
will not win one, and that does not make their research any less real.
